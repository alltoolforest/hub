Application Photo & Signature Resizer

Prepare online application photographs, signatures and supporting images: enter exact pixel dimensions and KB requirements, crop without stretching, and export JPG, PNG or WebP. A single-page A4 PDF is available for supporting images. Verify your application portal’s rules before submitting.

Your image stays on your device.

For this tool, photo, signature and PDF conversion run in your browser using processing libraries served by AllToolForest. Uploaded image content is not sent to our server.
