Click pages below to keep them (Shift-click selects a range). Selected pages are saved in a new PDF.

Each range becomes its own PDF. Use commas to add more: 1-3, 5, 8-. Keywords: last; open ends like 8- run to the final page; 5-1 reverses.

Cut the document into equal parts. Use 1 to save every page as a separate PDF.

Split into approximately equal parts.

Fill each file with as many pages as fit under the limit. A single page larger than the limit gets its own file. Large documents take longer.

Split at every top-level bookmark. Pages before the first bookmark go into a "front matter" file.

Click the pages you want to delete. Everything else is saved in a new PDF.

Complex PDF features may not survive page extraction.
