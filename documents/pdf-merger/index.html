PDF Merger

Combine two or more PDFs into one file. Add them in any order, rearrange them, then download the merged PDF.

📥
Drop PDF files here
or click to choose multiple files
Privacy: this tool reads and combines the PDFs in your browser using pdf-lib. The selected PDF contents are not intentionally uploaded by this tool.

How to merge PDFs

  1. Select two or more PDF files.
  2. Use the arrow buttons to put them in the order you want.
  3. Choose “Merge & download PDF”.
