How to convert a scanned PDF to text

  1. Choose a PDF from your device.
  2. Choose all pages or enter a page range within the displayed processing limits.
  3. Recognize the text, then review names, numbers and uncertain pages against the original.
  4. Copy the reviewed text or download a UTF-8 TXT file.

Supported documents and limitations

Printed English is the supported OCR language. Selectable digital text may be extracted directly. Mixed-content pages may need manual correction of reading order or duplicate text. Handwriting, exact layout reconstruction and searchable PDF export are not supported. Current input limits are 15 MB and 10 pages per run on narrow screens, and 40 MB and 30 pages per run on wider screens. These limits are not guarantees for unusually complex documents.

For password-protected PDFs, use a trusted PDF application to unlock a copy first. Recognition quality depends on scan quality and device resources.

For PDFs with selectable text, see Extract PDF Text. For standalone images, use Image to Text OCR.
