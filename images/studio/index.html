Free browser-based image editor for everyday image tasks

Image Studio combines the image jobs people most often need before uploading, sharing or publishing a picture: resize dimensions, crop to a useful aspect ratio, rotate or flip, adjust brightness, contrast and saturation, compress file size, convert formats and add a text watermark.

Compress an image to a maximum KB size

Enter a maximum size such as 50 KB, 100 KB, 200 KB or 500 KB. JPG and supported WebP exports first reduce encoding quality while keeping the chosen dimensions. If the target is still too small, you can explicitly allow Image Studio to reduce dimensions. The tool reports when a requested target cannot be reached instead of silently pretending it succeeded.

Resize for social, web and application uploads

Use custom pixel dimensions or quick presets for square, portrait, vertical, thumbnail and landscape layouts. Common crop ratios including 1:1, 4:5, 16:9, 9:16, 3:2 and 4:3 are available, and the crop coordinates can also be entered by keyboard.

Supported formats and browser notes

  • Open JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, HEIF, AVIF and BMP images.
  • Export JPG and PNG in all supported modern browsers. WebP export appears only when the browser can encode it correctly.
  • HEIC and HEIF use the decoder bundled with AllToolForest. AVIF uses the browser's native decoder, so older browsers may need JPG or PNG instead.
  • Large images are limited according to device memory and canvas constraints. iPhone and iPad exports use a conservative 4,096-pixel side limit for reliability.

Privacy and metadata

Your selected image is processed in page memory. The image itself is not uploaded to an AllToolForest server. Because exports are newly encoded from canvas pixels, EXIF, GPS and camera metadata are not intentionally copied into the new file.

Image Studio FAQ

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Image Studio processes the selected image in your browser and creates the export locally.

Can I compress an image to 100 KB?

Yes. Set 100 KB as the maximum target. For difficult images, optionally allow dimension reduction so the tool has another way to meet the target.

Can I keep a PNG transparent?

Yes. Choose PNG and leave the canvas background on Automatic or Transparent. If you export as JPG, transparent areas become white because JPG does not support transparency.

Does Image Studio remove GPS information?

Exports are newly encoded from image pixels and do not intentionally preserve EXIF or GPS metadata.
