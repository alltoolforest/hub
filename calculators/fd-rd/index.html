FD and RD are different

Fixed / term deposit (FD / CD): one deposit grows for the selected tenure. Cumulative FD reinvests interest; payout FD distributes interest instead.

Recurring deposit / monthly saver: every monthly installment has a different time remaining until maturity, so each installment is accrued separately.

Rate adjustments

If your bank offers an additional senior-citizen or special rate, enter the published base rate and add the extra rate separately.
