How to improve your LinkedIn profile

Use the LinkedIn Profile Helper to review your current wording, compare it with a target role, and create editable suggestions for your headline, About section, experience and skills. The tool is designed to strengthen wording without inventing employers, skills, achievements or metrics.

How to write a strong LinkedIn headline

Use a clear target role or professional identity, then add only skills, specialties or evidence that genuinely describe your experience. LinkedIn currently allows up to 220 characters in the headline, so clarity matters more than stuffing every possible keyword.

How to improve your LinkedIn About section

Start with who you are professionally, explain your focus, support it with real skills or achievements, and end with the direction you are pursuing. LinkedIn currently allows up to 2,600 characters in the About section.

How to improve LinkedIn experience descriptions

Turn vague responsibilities into clear action-based statements while preserving the facts. Add outcomes only when you can support them. Never invent numbers, tools, employers or achievements.

Choosing LinkedIn skills

Separate skills you can support with your experience from target-role skills you may want to learn or verify. A suggested keyword should never be presented as a skill you already possess.

Using target-role keywords

Relevant role terminology can improve clarity and alignment, but keyword stuffing weakens readability. Use a term only when it accurately describes your work, knowledge or verified experience.

LinkedIn profiles for freshers and students

Focus on your target role, supported skills, projects, education and practical evidence. Avoid presenting coursework or interest as professional employment experience.

LinkedIn profiles for career changers

Keep your existing background visible while highlighting transferable evidence that supports the new direction. Do not rename unrelated skills or roles to make them look equivalent.

Writing truthful achievements

Use metrics only when they come from information you can verify. Strong wording should improve clarity, not manufacture performance percentages, certifications, years of experience or responsibilities.

Privacy

Profile text is processed in your browser. Saved drafts use this browser's local storage on this device. The core tool does not require LinkedIn login or profile scraping.

Frequently asked questions

Does this tool edit my LinkedIn account?

No. It creates editable suggestions that you can review and copy. It does not log in to LinkedIn or modify your profile automatically.

Will it invent skills or achievements for me?

No. Generated claims use the facts you provide. Target-role skills are shown separately as suggestions to review and should only be added if they are accurate.

Can I use it for a career change?

Yes. Career-changer mode keeps your existing background separate from the target role and focuses on truthful transferable evidence.

Where is my saved draft stored?

The saved draft is stored in this browser's local storage on this device until you clear it.
