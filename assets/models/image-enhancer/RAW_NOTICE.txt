AllToolForest Image Enhancer camera RAW support

Runtime wrapper: rawconvert-wasm 0.1.1 (MIT)
Underlying RAW engine: LibRaw 0.22.x (dual LGPL-2.1 / CDDL-1.0)
Runtime is loaded from jsDelivr and executes locally in a Web Worker.
Supported families include CR2, CR3, NEF/NRW, ARW/SRF/SR2, DNG, RAF, ORF, RW2, PEF, SRW, ERF, KDC, DCR, MOS, 3FR, IIQ, RWL, MEF, MRW and X3F.

No user image bytes are uploaded by AllToolForest; RAW decoding happens in the browser.
