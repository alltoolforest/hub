How the freelance rate estimate works

The calculator starts with the personal income you want available, adds a user-entered tax planning reserve and your business expenses, then compares that annual revenue requirement with the hours you realistically expect to bill clients.

Billable time is usually lower than total working time because freelancers also spend time on proposals, administration, marketing, bookkeeping, training and other non-client work. Reducing working weeks for holidays or planned time away also lowers annual billable capacity and therefore raises the sustainable rate.

The minimum hourly rate is the calculated floor under your assumptions. The recommended rate adds the contingency or profit buffer you choose. The client-day rate multiplies the hourly rate by your selected billable client-day hours.

Important: the tax reserve is only a planning assumption. This calculator does not apply country-specific tax law, benefits, payment fees, market pricing or legal requirements.
