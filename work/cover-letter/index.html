How the Cover Letter Builder works

Add your details, resume text or a PDF/DOCX resume, and the target job description. The builder uses the information you provide to create an editable cover letter matched to the role.

How to tailor a cover letter to a job description

Focus on the employer's required responsibilities and qualifications, then connect them to evidence already supported by your experience, education, projects, certifications or transferable skills.

What to include in a cover letter

A useful cover letter normally includes a clear greeting, the target role and company, relevant evidence, a truthful reason for your interest and a professional closing.

Cover letters for freshers

Freshers can use education, projects, internships, volunteering, certifications and transferable skills instead of inventing work experience.

Cover letters for experienced professionals

Experienced applicants should prioritize directly relevant achievements, responsibilities and measurable outcomes already supported by their resume.

Cover letter vs resume

A resume summarizes your background. A cover letter explains why selected parts of that background are relevant to one specific opportunity.

Recommended cover letter length

A concise cover letter may be around 180–250 words. A standard letter may be around 250–400 words when there is enough relevant evidence to support it.

Privacy

The builder is designed for browser-local generation. Drafts saved with the Save Draft control are stored in this browser on this device.

Frequently asked questions

Does the builder invent experience or achievements?

No. Its generation rules are designed to use candidate information supplied through the resume or form inputs.

Can a fresher create a cover letter?

Yes. Freshers can use education, projects, internships, volunteering, certifications and transferable skills.

Should I copy my whole resume into a cover letter?

No. A cover letter should select the strongest evidence relevant to the target role instead of repeating the entire resume.
